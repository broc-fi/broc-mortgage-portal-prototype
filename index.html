The Broc Score (0–100): What It Means for Underwriters

A deterministic, auditable 5-pillar mortgage readiness score powered by Clara (audit) & Clive (remediation)

Unlike a generic credit bureau score (Ireland's CCR does not output a consumer FICO score), the Broc Score (0–100) measures real-time Irish mortgage underwriting pass probability across 6–12 months of verified transaction data, identity/residency checks, and Central Bank of Ireland rules.

Pillar 1: Stress-Tested Repayment Capacity & NDI Margin Max 30 Points

Measures verified monthly repayment capacity (Verified Rent + Consistent Savings + Expiring Loans) against the proposed mortgage repayment stressed at Offered Rate + 2.0%. Full 30 pts requires ≥115% stress coverage and passing Net Disposable Income (NDI) after standard Irish household living costs.

Pillar 2: 6-Month Account Conduct & Behavioural Hygiene Max 25 Points

Clara scans 100% of current, joint, savings, and neobank (Revolut/N26) statements. Deducts points for: unpaid direct debits (-10), unarranged overdrafts (-8), referral fees (-5), regular gambling/betting >1.5% of net pay (-10), or active BNPL/Klarna installments (-6).

Pillar 3: Income Stability, Contract Tenure & Revenue Match Max 20 Points

Cross-checks 3 months payslips + Revenue Employment Detail Summary (EDS) against actual salary bank credits. Confirms permanent employment, probation passed, ≥6–12 months tenure, or 2–3 years certified accounts + Form 11 + ROS Tax Clearance for self-employed.

Pillar 4: Deposit Source (AML), LTV & Central Credit Register Max 15 Points

Verifies ≥10% deposit (90% LTV max) with full Anti-Money Laundering (AML) provenance: organic monthly savings build-up, Revenue Help-to-Buy (HTB) access code, or signed Gift Letter with donor statement proof, plus zero undisclosed loans or arrears on the CCR.

Pillar 5: Identity (KYC), Citizenship/Residency & Pack Completeness Max 10 Points

Biometric Passport/Driving Licence KYC, Proof of Address (<3 mos), verified PPSN document, and right-to-reside eligibility (Irish/UK/EU/EEA Citizen, or Non-EEA Stamp 4 / Critical Skills Employment Permit with required Irish residency history).

Complete 18-Point Underwriting Criteria: A) Mortgage in Principle vs B) Full Mortgage

Every identity, citizenship, tax, conduct, property, and legal requirement packaged by Broc

A) MORTGAGE IN PRINCIPLE (AIP) — 10 CORE CRITERIA

Identity, Residency, Tax, 6-Mo Conduct & Affordability

  1. Photo ID & Biometric Liveness (KYC): Valid Passport or Irish Driving Licence.
  2. Citizenship & Residency Status: Irish/UK/EU/EEA Citizen, or Non-EEA Stamp 4 / Critical Skills Permit.
  3. Proof of Current Address (AML): Utility bill or bank statement (<3–6 months).
  4. PPSN Verification: Official Revenue/DSP document confirming PPS Number.
  5. Central Credit Register (CCR) & Judgments: Zero undisclosed loans, arrears, or Stubbs/ISI Insolvency flags.
  6. Employment Status & Probation: Permanent PAYE role (probation passed) or ≥2 yrs Self-Employed.
  7. Verified Income & Tax Compliance: 3 mos payslips + Revenue EDS (or 2–3 yrs Audited Accounts + Form 11 + Tax Clearance).
  8. 6-Month Bank & Savings Statements: All current, savings & Revolut/N26 accounts via Yapily / CSV.
  9. Account Conduct & Red-Flag Screen: 0 unpaid DDs, 0 overdrafts, gambling <1.5%, BNPL/Klarna cleared.
  10. Deposit Source (AML) & +2% Stress Test: ≥10% deposit proven (Savings/HTB/Gift Letter) + Repayment Capacity ≥ Rate + 2%.
B) FULL MORTGAGE APPLICATION — +8 POST-"SALE AGREED" CRITERIA

Employer Sign-Off, Valuation, BER, Legal & Insurance

  1. Standardized Lender Salary Certificate: Employer HR signed & stamped form confirming base pay & standing.
  2. Pre-Offer Statement & Payslip Refresh: Latest month bank feed confirming continued savings & zero new debt.
  3. Sale Agreed Sales Advice Note & Booking Deposit: Estate agent confirmation of Sale Agreed price & paid booking deposit receipt.
  4. Lender Panel Property Valuation Report: Approved panel valuer inspection confirming market value ≥ Sale Agreed price.
  5. SEAI BER Certificate & Green Eligibility: Official BER cert (A1–B3 unlocks BOI EcoSaver / Green discounted rates).
  6. Structural Survey & Planning Compliance: Engineer survey (pre-1980 / rural) & Architect Opinion of Compliance.
  7. Solicitor Undertaking & Contracts for Sale: Approved panel solicitor undertaking, clean title deeds & signed contracts.
  8. Mortgage Protection, Home Insurance & SEPA DD: Bound Life Cover assigned to lender + Buildings Insurance (via Broc Tender) + signed Direct Debit mandate.
