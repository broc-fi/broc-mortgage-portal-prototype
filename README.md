# Broc Mortgage Underwriting & Readiness Portal (Prototype)

Lightweight white-label mortgage provider portal prototype (`Bank of Ireland` tenant vs `Broc Generic White-Label` template).

## Included Features
- **Tenant Switcher:** Toggle between **Bank of Ireland (BOI)** and **Broc Generic White-Label** rules, rates, and branding.
- **5-Pillar Broc Score (0–100):**
  1. Repayment Capacity & +2.0% Stress-Test Margin (30 pts)
  2. 6-Month Account Conduct & Behavioural Hygiene (25 pts)
  3. Income Stability, Tenure & Revenue EDS Match (20 pts)
  4. Deposit AML Source, LTV & Clean CCR (15 pts)
  5. Identity (KYC), Citizenship / Stamp 4 Residency & Pack Completeness (10 pts)
- **Full 18-Criteria Irish Mortgage Checklist:**
  - **Stage A — Mortgage in Principle (AIP):** 10 criteria (Photo ID KYC, Citizenship/Residency, Proof of Address, PPSN, Central Credit Register, Employment/Probation, Payslips/EDS or Form 11/Tax Clearance, 6-Mo Statements, Account Conduct, Deposit AML & Stress Test).
  - **Stage B — Full Mortgage Application:** +8 post-Sale-Agreed criteria (Stamped Salary Cert, Statement Refresh, Sales Advice Note, Panel Valuation, SEAI BER Cert, Structural Survey, Solicitor Undertaking, Bound Mortgage Protection & Home Insurance).
